STATE-LEVEL BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
Factory-to-Customer Shipping Route Efficiency Analysis
Nassau Candy Distributor
============================================================

1. INVENTORY ALLOCATION
   Prioritize safety stock in California, New York, Texas — these states generate the highest cumulative sales. Aligning warehouse buffer levels with seasonal demand peaks in these markets will reduce stockout risk.

2. CARRIER OPTIMIZATION
   Renegotiate carrier SLAs or introduce alternative carriers for Wyoming, Vermont, Prince Edward Island, Saskatchewan, Maine — these states consistently record the longest average shipping lead times and are prime candidates for expedited or dedicated lane agreements.

3. DELAY REDUCTION
   States with delay rates above 35%: Wyoming, Vermont, Newfoundland and Labrador, Prince Edward Island, Maine. Introduce real-time shipment tracking, buffer days for Standard Class routes into these states, and set up proactive exception alerts for orders approaching the SLA breach threshold.

4. WAREHOUSE PLANNING
   Evaluate regional distribution center (DC) placement near California, New York, Texas, Pennsylvania, Washington to reduce last-mile lead times. A DC positioned centrally within the highest-volume cluster could simultaneously serve multiple top-10 states and reduce cross-country freight costs.

5. ROUTE OPTIMIZATION
   Review factory-to-state routing for Wyoming, Vermont, Prince Edward Island, Saskatchewan, Maine. Shifting volume from Standard Class to Second Class on these routes, or introducing direct factory-to-DC consolidation lanes, may yield meaningful lead-time reductions without proportional cost increases.

6. COST REDUCTION
   Total cost concentration is highest in California, New York, Texas, Pennsylvania, Washington. Conduct a cost-per-unit benchmark against comparable routes and identify consolidation opportunities (e.g., full-truckload vs. LTL) to lower per-shipment costs in these markets.

7. REGIONAL EXPANSION
   Low-penetration states: Wyoming, Saskatchewan, West Virginia, Nova Scotia, North Dakota. Assess whether these markets represent genuinely low demand or are simply under-served by current distribution infrastructure. A targeted promotional campaign combined with reduced shipping thresholds could stimulate incremental volume.

8. RESOURCE ALLOCATION
   Direct field sales and customer success resources towards Ohio, Colorado, California, Virginia, New York — the top composite-ranked states that combine strong revenue, low delay rates and high route efficiency. Protecting and expanding share in best-performing markets delivers the highest return on resource investment.
